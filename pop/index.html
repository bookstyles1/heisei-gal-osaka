What's gyaru? / ギャルとは

The loudest girls of the Heisei era

Gyaru (from the English "gal") was a street fashion that took over Japan in the late '90s and 2000s, part of the Heisei era (1989–2019). It started on the streets of Shibuya and spread across the country through gal magazines and the shops of Shibuya 109.

Gyaru broke every rule of "proper" Japanese beauty: tanned skin, bleached hair, huge lashes, glitter, and attitude to match. It was about having fun with your friends and looking exactly how you wanted.

Osaka had its own scene too, centered on Amemura (America-mura), the city's youth and vintage-clothing district. That's where your day starts.

Ganguroガングロ
Deep tan, white eye and lip makeup, bleached hair. The boldest look of the late '90s.
Shiro-gal白ギャル
The fair-skinned 2000s style with big eyes, pink cheeks and long curled hair.
Loose socksルーズソックス
Extra-long white socks scrunched down to the ankles. The school-girl gyaru signature.
Purikuraプリクラ
Photo-sticker booths with decorated frames. Every gyaru pose comes from here.
Decoデコ
Rhinestones and stickers on everything: flip phones, nails, mirrors.

Good to know / FAQ

Before you book

Sensitive skin?

We use single-use applicators and tell you what goes on your face. Let us know about allergies when you book.

Rainy day?

We move the shoot to covered shopping streets and indoor spots. Your gal day still happens.

Your size

Send your height, clothing size and shoe size after booking so your outfit is ready when you arrive.

Running late?

Message us on WhatsApp. We keep a buffer, but very late arrivals may get a shorter shoot.

Do I need to speak Japanese?

No problem. Translation apps are amazing these days, and we add gestures and simple English. Some of our staff speak fluent English, so it depends on who's on duty that day. Everyone on our team takes great care of you, so come as you are and enjoy Japanese culture to the fullest!

Can guys or non-binary guests join?

Yes. Gyaru-o (the male version of gyaru) is part of the culture too. Everyone is welcome.

Can I bring my own camera or phone?

Of course. Your friends can film behind the scenes while our photographer shoots.

Where do I return the outfit?

At the drop-off point we give you on the day, by 8PM. It's in the Minami area, close to the station.

Can I change my date?

Yes. After payment we email you a booking confirmation with a link to change your date. You can move to another open slot up to 7 days before your booking. Refunds are not available.

What's the cancellation policy?

Once payment is complete, we can't offer refunds for any reason.
